# 영상 대본 (쉬운 단어 버전)

목표: **4~5분**. 화면 녹화 + 나레이션.
형식 — **[ ] = 무엇을 할지 (한국어)**, **" " = 말할 것 (쉬운 영어)**.
대사는 그대로 안 읽어도 됩니다. 편하게 본인 말로 바꿔도 OK. (한국어로 하셔도 되고요.)

> 녹화 전: 페이지 **새로고침**(승인 데모 초기화), 창 **최대화**, 배포 링크 열기.

---

## 0. 시작 (15초)

[대시보드 화면(`/`)에서 시작]

> "This is Ledgerline. It's a tool for tax accountants, built for the case study.
> I focused on four things, and they all come together on one main screen."

---

## 1. 대시보드 (45초)

[상단 숫자 4개를 가리키며]

> "The dashboard is built to show me what to work on right now.
> These four numbers each point to something I need to act on. They're not just for show."

[**"Waiting on your approval"** 밴드를 가리키며]

> "Here, the AI wants to change a number, but it needs my okay first.
> I keep these separate because they're a different kind of task. I'll come back to it."

[**Manager** 버튼 클릭]

> "A manager sees a different view: who on the team has too much on their plate."

[**My queue** 또는 **All firm**으로 돌아오기]

> "And it still works fine with over 200 returns. I can search, filter, and page through them."

---

## 2. 워크벤치 — 숫자의 출처 보기 (75초)

[승인 밴드의 **Marcus & Elena Delgado** 클릭]

[상단 상태 바 가리키며]

> "Every return moves through the same six steps."

[**Wages** 필드 클릭 → 오른쪽 패널 가리키며]

> "This number is $181,300. The panel on the right shows exactly where it came from:
> the two W-2 forms, with the exact box marked, and how they were added up."

[**Capital gain** 필드 클릭]

> "This one comes from two different forms, and it even tells me the page: page 4 of 6.
> The AI flagged it, because some cost info is missing. That's why it's not sure about this one."

[헤더 오른쪽 **Legend** 버튼 클릭 → 팝업 보여주고 닫기]

> "Every value tells me three simple things: where it came from,
> how much I can trust it, and whether I can edit it. This little guide is on every screen."

[**Standard deduction** 필드 클릭]

> "This one is locked, and it tells me why. It's set by law, so I can't change it."

---

## 3. AI 승인 — 핵심 장면 (60초)

[**Estimated tax payments** 필드 클릭]

> "This is my favorite part. The client said they paid $6,000 during the year.
> The AI checked the official IRS record and only found $4,500. So it wants to lower the number."

[승인 패널의 Current / Proposed / Impact 가리키며]

> "It shows the old value, the new value, and the effect.
> But nothing has changed yet. The AI can suggest, but it can't change the return by itself."

[상단 **Refund** 값을 가리킨 뒤 → **Approve change** 클릭]

> "Watch the refund at the top. I click approve..."

[환급액이 바뀌는 것 가리키며]

> "...and it updates right away, from $10,818 down to $9,318. It also saves who made the decision.
> The totals are really being calculated. Nothing here is faked."

---

## 4. 문서 (30초)

[사이드바 **Documents** 클릭]

> "Same look and feel here. This is the third screen using the same system."

[**1099-B** 문서 클릭 → "Feeds N return lines" 가리키며]

> "This works the other way too: from a document, I can see which numbers on the return use it.
> That's handy when a client sends a corrected form."

[리스트의 한 줄 클릭 → 워크벤치로 점프]

> "And it takes me straight to that line."

---

## 5. Client view (30초)

[다시 리턴 화면으로. 오른쪽 상단 **Client view** 토글 클릭]

> "Same return, but this is what the client sees.
> The AI scores and internal notes are hidden. The client doesn't need those."

[**Firm view**로 다시 토글]

> "One product, two audiences."

---

## 6. 마무리 (20초)

> "To be clear: there's no real AI or document scanning here. That part is faked, like the brief asked.
> But the math, the ranking, and everything you clicked are real. Thanks for watching."

---

### 팁
- 대본 그대로 읽지 말고, 옆 화면에 띄워두고 **자연스럽게**.
- 한 번에 완벽하게 하려 하지 말 것 — 살짝 버벅여도 그냥 진행.
- 막히면: 대시보드 → 승인 밴드 → 승인 → 환급 바뀜, 이 흐름만 확실히 보여주면 절반은 성공.
- 어려운 단어 없이 짧게. 천천히 말해도 4~5분이면 충분합니다.
