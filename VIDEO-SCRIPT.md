# 영상 대본 (간단 버전)

목표: **4~5분**. 화면 녹화 + 나레이션.
형식 — **[ ] = 무엇을 할지 (한국어)**, **" " = 말할 것 (영어, 짧게)**.
대사는 그대로 안 읽어도 됩니다. 편하게 본인 말로 바꿔도 OK.

> 녹화 전: 페이지 **새로고침**(승인 데모 초기화), 창 **최대화**, 배포 링크 열기.

---

## 0. 시작 (15초)

[대시보드 화면(`/`)에서 시작]

> "This is Ledgerline — a tax platform I built for the case study.
> I focused on four things: traceability, the AI, the affordance system, and the dashboard."

---

## 1. 대시보드 (45초)

[상단 지표 4개를 가리키며]

> "The dashboard answers one question: what should I work on right now?
> These four numbers are all decisions, not vanity stats."

[**"Waiting on your approval"** 밴드를 가리키며]

> "The AI proposed a change here. It's separate from the deadline queue —
> it's a different kind of decision."

[**Manager** 버튼 클릭]

> "A manager sees a different view — who on the team is overloaded."

[**All firm** 또는 **My queue**로 돌아오기]

> "And it stays usable with 240 returns — search, filters, pagination."

---

## 2. 워크벤치 — 추적성 + 어포던스 (75초)

[승인 밴드의 **Marcus & Elena Delgado** 클릭]

[상단 상태 바 가리키며]

> "Every return has the same six-step status."

[**Wages** 필드 클릭 → 오른쪽 패널 가리키며]

> "This number is $181,300. Where's it from?
> It shows the source W-2s with the exact box highlighted, and how they were summed."

[**Capital gain** 필드 클릭]

> "This one's built from two documents, and it tells me the exact page — page 4 of 6.
> The AI flagged it: cost basis is missing on some lots. That's why confidence is low."

[헤더 오른쪽 **Legend** 버튼 클릭 → 팝오버 보여주고 닫기]

> "Every value is described by three things: where it came from, how much to trust it,
> and what I can do with it. This legend is on every screen."

[**Standard deduction** 필드 클릭]

> "This one's locked — and it tells me why. Set by law, can't edit it."

---

## 3. AI 승인 — 핵심 장면 (60초)

[**Estimated tax payments** 필드 클릭]

> "This is my favorite part. The client said they paid $6,000 in estimates.
> The AI checked the IRS transcript and only found $4,500 — so it's proposing a change."

[승인 패널의 Current / Proposed / Impact 가리키며]

> "It shows the current value, the proposed value, and the impact.
> But nothing has changed yet — the AI can propose, it can't edit the return."

[상단 **Refund** 값을 가리킨 뒤 → **Approve change** 클릭]

> "Watch the refund. I approve..."

[환급액이 바뀌는 것 가리키며]

> "...and it recalculates live, $10,818 down to $9,318. It also logs who decided.
> The totals are really computed — nothing's hardcoded."

---

## 4. 문서 (30초)

[사이드바 **Documents** 클릭]

> "Same visual language here — a third screen, same system."

[**1099-B** 문서 클릭 → "Feeds N return lines" 가리키며]

> "This is traceability in reverse: from a document, which lines depend on it?
> Useful when an amended form comes in."

[리스트의 한 줄 클릭 → 워크벤치로 점프]

> "And it jumps me straight to that line."

---

## 5. Client view (30초)

[다시 리턴 화면으로. 오른쪽 상단 **Client view** 토글 클릭]

> "Same return, but this is what the client sees.
> The confidence scores and internal flags are gone — they don't need those."

[**Firm view**로 다시 토글]

> "One product, two audiences."

---

## 6. 마무리 (20초)

> "To be clear: there's no real AI or OCR — that's simulated, like the brief said.
> But the math, the prioritization, and every interaction are real.
> Thanks for watching."

---

### 팁
- 대본 그대로 읽지 말고, 옆 화면에 띄워두고 **자연스럽게**.
- 한 번에 완벽하게 하려 하지 말 것 — 살짝 버벅여도 그냥 진행.
- 막히면: 대시보드 → 승인 밴드 → 승인 → 환급 바뀜, 이 흐름만 확실히 보여주면 절반은 성공.
